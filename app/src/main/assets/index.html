How to Setup & Use

  1. Pair Windows Laptop: Open phone Bluetooth settings and pair with your Windows PC as an HID input device.
  2. Save Your Password: Go to Settings tab, type your exact Windows login password (numbers or spaces allowed), and click Save Password.
  3. Select Bluetooth Target: Choose your laptop from the Bluetooth Device dropdown list in the Settings tab.
  4. Set Up App PIN: Create a 4-6 digit PIN to secure this app from unauthorized access.
  5. Unlock Laptop: Navigate to the Unlock tab, tap Unlock Laptop, and scan your fingerprint to send the rapid keystroke sequence.
